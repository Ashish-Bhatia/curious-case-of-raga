## Claim and context
The Indian National Congress's [27 August 2025 edited account of the 7 August briefing](https://inc.in/congress-sandesh/national/a-systematic-multi-pronged-approach-the-bjp-controlled-eci) reports that “approximately 1,00,250 votes were stolen out of 6.5 lakh total votes in Mahadevapura.” Attribution is to the official-party briefing summary, **not yet certified as a verbatim unedited video passage**.

The account describes five purported categories:
| Category | Count claimed |
| --- | ---: |
| Duplicate voters | 11,965 |
| Fake or invalid addresses | 40,009 |
| Bulk registrations at single addresses | 10,452 |
| No or invalid photos | 4,132 |
| Misuse of Form 6 | 33,692 |
| **Sum** | **100,250** |

The arithmetic is exact. It alone does not show five mutually exclusive sets, unique invalid electors, impersonation, actual votes cast, partisan beneficiaries or a changed electoral outcome.

## Independently testable propositions
1. Each category includes genuine, legally improper electoral-roll entries, validated at the individual level.
2. The categories do not overlap and total 100,250 unique improper entries.
3. Each improper entry corresponds to an unlawfully cast or counted vote in the 2024 election.
4. These votes benefited the BJP enough to change the Bangalore Central result.
5. The ECI and BJP knowingly colluded in such manipulation.

These are different propositions, and positive proof of one is not proof of the others.

## Official 2024 denominator and outcome cross-check (15 October 2026)

The [Election Commission of India, General Election 2024 statistical report, *Assembly Segment-wise Information: Electors*, Table 15, printed page 79 / PDF page 79](https://www.eci.gov.in/eci-backend/public/all_files/GE-2024-statistical-report/15-Assembly-SegmentWise-Information-Electors.pdf) records **659,826 registered electors** and **354,830 EVM votes polled including NOTA** in Mahadevapura (part of Bangalore Central). The screenshot and extracted printed table were inspected. These figures establish the critical distinction: "about 6.5 lakh" describes **electors**, not **votes actually cast**. The EVM column excludes any postal votes, so do not call 354,830 the complete final all-mode vote count.

The ECI's [2024 *Constituency-wise Detailed Results* official report, Bangalore Central constituency 25](https://www.eci.gov.in/eci-backend/public/all_files/GE-2024-statistical-report/33-Constituency-Wise-Detailed-Result.pdf) is indexed with candidate totals **658,915 (P. C. Mohan, BJP)** and **626,208 (Mansoor Ali Khan, INC)**. Subtraction gives a **32,707-vote** winning margin. The search-index text was retrieved, but a complete direct PDF fetch did not succeed in this run. Independently reporting the margin is valid; deriving a changed outcome from 100,250 suspect entries without legally and statistically defensible unique-voter/ballot matching is not.

The claimant's possible use of "6.5 lakh votes" to mean total voter-list entries should be checked against unedited video. It would be an error to interpret that phrase unambiguously as the number of ballots cast without establishing context.

## Finding and inference controls
- **Supported component:** Some address entries in the rolls did not match the people living at those addresses when journalists visited in August 2025.
- **Important qualification:** August 2025 occupancy alone cannot retroactively prove eligibility or residence on the relevant 2024 electoral-roll cut-off date.
- **Unsupported leap:** A stale/duplicate/malformed roll entry does not prove anyone voted illegally, let alone voted for a specific candidate. Ballot secrecy prevents attributing particular votes to a party without evidence.
- **Unverified calculation:** The five category counts sum to 100,250, but any overlap and classification error rates are unknown.
- **Undecided charge:** Neither a CEO request for an affidavit nor a political rejection of that request decides whether fraud occurred.
- **Provisional verdict:** **Unsubstantiated** for the precise theft and collusion proposition, pending the missing material. Not **False** because the field evidence does show real registration anomalies warranting investigation.

## Counterargument and strongest plausible reading
Gandhi may have used “stolen votes” colloquially to mean suspicious or invalid electoral-roll **registrations**, not 100,250 ballot papers actually cast. On that interpretation the numerical claim still needs record-level verification, but contemporaneous ground reporting makes blanket dismissal improper. Migrant workers' histories, shared addresses and slow deletion rules offer benign explanations for part of the list, but do not remove the need for individual verification.
