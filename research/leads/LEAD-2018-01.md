# LEAD-2018-01 — September 2018 Rafale accusations and separate defamation proceedings

**Status:** Research lead only. **First recorded:** 15 October 2026, checkpoint RAGA-20261015-011. **No factual verdict.** Original claimant media and contract evidence incomplete. **Do not conflate with RAGA-2019-0001** (the unrelated 10 April 2019 allegation that the Supreme Court itself endorsed an accusation).

## Identified original assertions (not yet fully authenticated from claimant's recordings)

1. **20 September 2018, Sagwara/Dungarpur, Rajasthan rally.** The Bombay High Court's 8 September 2026 order (WP 4391/2021), paragraphs 3–4, recites the *complainant's* allegation that Gandhi used the Hindi campaign slogan “Gali gali mein shor hai, Hindustan ka chowkidar chor hai.” This is a quoted pleading reproduced within a judicial order, **not independently authenticated end-to-end footage**. Contemporaneous [Mango News 20 September 2018 rally video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fy9HYh3jFr0) is located, but uninterrupted playback/transcript/timecodes were not checked.
2. **24 September 2018, Twitter/X post.** The same court order, paragraphs 5–6, attributes to Gandhi: “The sad truth about India's Commander-in-Thief.” Contemporaneous [India Today article](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/rahul-gandhi-rafale-deal-francois-hollande-mediapart-1347658-2018-09-24) embeds the claimant's dated post with `pic.twitter.com/USrxqlJTWe`. The direct original X status ID and attached video were **not authenticated** in this run. These are allegations/rhetoric, not a finding that theft occurred.
3. **25 September 2018, Amethi speech.** The Bombay High Court order, paragraph 8, quotes the complainant's allegation involving a payment of Rs 20,000 crore to Anil Ambani and other accusations. Treat all amounts and conduct as **unverified allegations** until original speech video and procurement/financial records are tested. The court order does not decide their truth.

## Litigation provenance and limits

- **Underlying proceedings:** Complaint C.C. 67/MISC/2018, magistrate order of 28 August 2019, Bombay High Court Criminal Writ Petition No. 4391/2021. The [judicial text reproduced by Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/167473602/) identifies Justice N.R. Borkar's **8 September 2026** decision. The record appears as court-authored original text on a third-party host; **official HC PDF signature was not validated**.
- **8 September 2026 High Court outcome:** Paragraphs 17–23 address whether the complainant was a “person aggrieved” under CrPC section 199 and whether the process should be quashed. The High Court declined to interfere, expressly leaving important questions for trial. **This is a threshold procedural ruling, neither conviction nor proof of theft/defamation**.
- **13 October 2026 Supreme Court appeal:** The [Supreme Court's official latest-orders index](https://www.sci.gov.in/latest-orders/) lists *Rahul Gandhi v State of Maharashtra*, SLP(Crl) No. **19559/2026**, Diary **61668/2026**, order dated 13 October, uploaded 14 October. This is **primary official case/order metadata**. The downloadable signed order body was not retrieved.
- **Reported interim stay:** [The Indian Express, 14 October 2026](https://indianexpress.com/article/legal-news/rahul-gandhi-defamation-case-pm-modi-supreme-court-trial-stay-10919174/) reports the Supreme Court stayed lower-court trial proceedings and issued notice to Maharashtra. It attributes the original complaint to Gandhi's **2018** remarks, unlike some reports describing them as 2019. The **2018 speech date** is supported by paragraphs 3 and 5 of the reproduced 2026 High Court order. Treat stay details as independently reported, pending examination of the authenticated Supreme Court order PDF.
- **Separate 2019 case:** RAGA-2019-0001 addresses *Meenakshi Lekhi v Rahul Gandhi*, Contempt Petition (Criminal) 3/2019, arising from Gandhi attributing “Chowkidar chor hai” to the Supreme Court after its 10 April 2019 Rafale document-admissibility decision. The 2019 Court's attribution clarification **does not resolve** 2018 political-slogan defamation proceedings or substantiate 2018 Rafale transaction allegations.

## Testable questions, not findings

- What did Gandhi say at Sagwara on 20 September 2018, in the precise original Hindi and uninterrupted context?
- Which factual claims, if any, accompany political labels such as “thief,” and what records would establish or refute each?
- Did the government direct the choice of an offset partner or transfer money alleged in September 2018? Compare **contemporaneous** Francoise Hollande / Mediapart interview, Dassault's dated response, Indian Ministry of Defence rebuttal and contract/off-sets/cost and payment records. Do not treat either side's declarations as dispositive.
- Does the original 24 September 2018 X post/media support all wording quoted in the subsequent complaint?
- What exactly did the signed Supreme Court **13 October 2026** interim order direct and does it cite a distinct scope? Do not equate an interim stay with exoneration.
- Underlying speech is political rhetoric in part. Do not assign one aggregated verdict to a slogan, quoted third-party allegation and discrete transaction fact.
