## Case and lead analysis, chronological order

### 9 June 2004: LEAD-2004-06-09 (updated)

- UPA policy commitment to repeal POTA remains **provisionally Supported** by the 27 May coalition programme, later parliamentary records and statute. This does not prove Cabinet-minute timing.
- Alleged POTA misuse now has meaningful retrospective corroboration: ministry figures show no prima facie POTA basis against many reviewed individuals. These are not automatically findings of deliberate or malicious prosecution, nor a denominator for all POTA registrations.
- Case-specific contrary result: July 2004 committee rejected misuse in identified Mumbai blast prosecutions, as documented by a 2010 judicial decision. That does not refute adverse review results in other cases.
- Claim about Manmohan Singh governing without alternative power centres remains **Unresolved**. Gandhi original June 9 audio and full context missing.
- Combined lead remains investigating without final case verdict or publication.

### 27 July 2004: LEAD-2004-07-27 (new)

- PTI attributes to Rahul Gandhi: “There is no governance in the state. It is only a drama.” The remark is primarily political rhetoric/opinion. PTI separately reports his criticism of deteriorating law and order, his reference to a Congress worker's murder, and complaints from local residents about electricity and roads.
- **Important attribution:** Complaints regarding power supply and roads belong to residents in PTI's account. Do not present them as a separate verbatim Rahul Gandhi quotation.
- **Electricity:** The September 2004 independent Prayas report retrospectively corroborates June–July state-wide supply problems. Supported context, moderate confidence; no exact local service hours or attribution of managerial blame established.
- **Murder:** Victim identity, event location, FIR, investigation and disposition **searched but not found**. Allegation remains unverified, not disproven.
- **Crime deterioration:** No authenticated month/district comparable police series with rates/reporting controls; no verdict established.
- **Roads:** No named road, official maintenance/inspection record or route survey recovered.
- **Overall:** investigating lead; no single final verdict. 'No governance' assessed as opinion, power-scarcity context supported, alleged murder and comparative crime trend unresolved/unsubstantiated.
