## Exact sources obtained or newly inspected in this run

1. **11–12 January 2004, authentic BJP-authored primary party resolution.** BJP National Executive Hyderabad, *Economic Resolution*, now in party library 2005 compilation and digitized 2016: https://library.bjp.org/jspui/bitstream/123456789/265/1/BJP-Economics%20Resolutions.pdf. **PDF pages 1,3 (indices 0,2), visually inspected**. It describes unresolved poverty, underdevelopment, planned manufacturing expansion and unfinished infrastructure despite glowing assessments of NDA achievements. Its later transcription/compilation does not by itself authenticate 2004 campaign broadcast creatives.
2. **22 February 2004, contemporary reporting:** Times of India on I&B *Achievements of NDA Government* campaign booklet, https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/india-shining-go-by-the-book/articleshow/511964.cms. Original booklet not retrieved.
3. **8 June 2004, contemporaneous claimant reportage:** PTI reproduction in Rediff, https://in.rediff.com/news/2004/jun/08rahul.htm. Reports complaints of attacks on Congress workers, concerns about law and order and Congress–SP coordination. **Original audio/transcript missing.**
4. **9 June 2004, contemporaneous claimant reportage:** PTI reproduction in Rediff, https://www.rediff.com/news/report/rahul/20040609.htm. Reports separate remarks on POTA repeal and abuse, claims of Manmohan Singh government autonomy, earlier NDA campaign. **Original audio missing**.
5. **27 May 2004 coalition policy, accessible reproduction:** UPA National Common Minimum Programme, https://architexturez.net/doc/az-cf-182356, Defence/Internal Security section. Promised to repeal POTA and alleged misuse. **First-party signed original not recovered**; the allegation itself is not independently substantiated by the coalition's assertion.
6. **14 July 2004 primary parliamentary source indexed but PDF inaccessible:** Rajya Sabha UQ339, https://www.rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/65782/1/PQ_202_14072004_U339_p59_p59.pdf, page 59, MoS Home Sriprakash Jaiswal. Search-indexed text quotes the CMP and indicates repeal action was initiated; direct PDF fetch **timed out**, so no claim the scanned page was visually examined.
7. **2004 primary statute metadata:** India Code *Prevention of Terrorism (Repeal) Act 2004*, https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1672?view_type=browse; Act 26, enacted 21 December 2004, effective 21 September 2004.
8. **25 November 2004 primary MHA government release:** https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?relid=5082. MHA affirms 21 September repeal ordinance and gives government's concern about POTA misuse as its justification. This is **official explanation**, not independent judicial proof of all alleged misuse.
9. **9 June 2004 response, political contemporary report:** https://in.rediff.com/news/2004/jun/08rahul1.htm; Samajwadi Party's Amar Singh response to Gandhi's comments, **not** independently verified crime data.
