- The original Supreme Court judgment rejected a real government procedural objection. Interpretations that say the Court sided with the government on **10 April** are incorrect. But rejection of a preliminary objection did not amount to a finding that Modi stole money. The separate [15 April Court clarification](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/13640/13640_2019_Order_15-Apr-2019.pdf) also disclaims such a judicial finding. The 2019 verdict remains confined to attribution; no claim of knowing deception is established.

A duplicated registration or suspicious address **is not automatically a ballot cast**. Legitimate mass tenancy and independent field evidence of anomalous addresses both deserve weight. The voter-denial reporting and official notice are adverse accounts, not substitutes for a forensic audit or judicial adjudication.
