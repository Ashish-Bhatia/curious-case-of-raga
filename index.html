05 — OUR EDITORIAL CODE

The joke is free.
The facts are not.

Political satire is commentary. Factual findings require records and traceable reasoning.

01

Find the original

Locate the full speech, post, interview, filing or parliamentary record. Preserve its date, language and context.

02

Test against evidence

Prioritise original data, legislation, signed orders and independent evidence. Scrutinise official rebuttals too.

03

Choose the right verdict

False, misleading, unsubstantiated, partly true, supported, true, disputed, unresolved or opinion. Never infer intent from error alone.

04

Expose the reasoning

Publish linked sources, relevant context, corrections and review date. Withdraw or revise any finding contradicted by new records.
